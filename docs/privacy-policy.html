Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Overview

Volocal is a fully on-device voice AI assistant. Your privacy is fundamental to how this app is built — all speech recognition, language model inference, and text-to-speech processing happen entirely on your device.

Data Collection

Volocal does not collect, store, transmit, or share conversation data.

  • Your voice input is processed entirely on your device and is never sent to any server.
  • Conversations are not recorded, logged, or stored beyond the current session.
  • No analytics, tracking, or telemetry SDKs are included.
  • No account or sign-in is required.

Microphone Access

Volocal requests microphone access solely to capture your voice for on-device speech-to-text processing. Audio data is processed in real-time and is not stored or transmitted.

Network Usage

Internet is used only to download (and optionally search/list) public AI model files from Hugging Face — CoreML speech/voice packs and GGUF language models you choose. After those files are on the device, voice chat works offline. Speech audio, transcripts, and model tokens are not uploaded.

Hugging Face may see your IP address and which public model files you fetch. That is ordinary HTTPS file download, not a voice API.

Third-Party Services

Volocal does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. Model weights are fetched from Hugging Face and (at compile time) source packages from GitHub.

Children's Privacy

Volocal does not collect any data from any users, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy is updated, the changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact
